# QC walk

Live: https://rydelicmusic.github.io/octave/park/

Do this in Walk. The eye starts near the Gate at (0, +230), looking north down the 14 m spine.

1. Read the clock on the right. It should say a time and OPEN. The land is not an empty canvas. Walk, 3rd, and Drone are still at the top.
2. Press Admit. The clock line should say ADMITTED. G is the comp key if you skip the button.
3. Press Block Coaster. The note should say you are queued, wait 0 min, and that you may board. That is the load slot, not a mid-course board.
4. Press Board. The view leaves the Gate and sits with the train.
5. Press Close restraint, then Dispatch. The status for Block Coaster goes to cycling. The block reads full. Restraints read closed.
6. Wait out the lap, or let it run. The train climbs the chain, drops, and returns to the station. Esc during the lap brakes along the rail and unloads at the station. It does not jump there.
7. At the station the restraint opens. Press the walk exit if you are still seated. The camera returns to Walk.
8. Press Board Wheel. If a gondola is not in the bottom window, the board is refused until the wheel is parked. Join that queue the same way: Admit is already done, so the button queues you.

Expected: the park stays populated, the hero lap is a closed ride, and the wheel is a separate queue. No lake, no hotel, no fifth land.
