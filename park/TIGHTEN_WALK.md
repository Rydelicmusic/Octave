# Tighten walk

Date: 2026-09-23 00:46 CDT
Start at the door, or use the documented bypass.

1. Open https://rydelicmusic.github.io/octave/park/door.html and press TICKET. You land on the park with `?admit=1`. The bypass, if you skip the door, is `park/index.html?dev=1`.
2. Gate. You are on the spine, looking toward the hub. The “Park Circuit · 25 min” board faces you. Walk / 3rd / Drone are on screen. The road is the road.
3. Look west. The Block crest is a lit tower, about 36 m, left of the spine and south of the hub. The rails are the thick pale ribbon under it. Four orange cars leave the station on their own while the park is open. Riders sit in those cars.
4. Board. The clock should say ADMITTED. Join Block Coaster, then BOARD. Esc puts you back on foot. The train still brakes on the rail.
5. Lap. The chain climbs to the crest, the drop is the fall after it, the helix brings the train around, and the brakes return it to the same station. The headless lap reached s 214 of 216 and dispatched again.
6. Refresh. The same browser still has the ticket, and the last ride id is the Block Coaster, if you boarded.

What this walk does not claim: a second coaster, a bigger wheel, a bigger swing ride, a parade on the spine, or a new lake.
