# QC2 walk

Live: https://rydelicmusic.github.io/octave/park/

Start in Walk at the Gate, looking north down the spine.

1. The street in front of you is the 14 m spine. It should feel level. There is no river in it and no trench.
2. Walk north a short way. The hub plaza ahead stays low. Walk, 3rd, and Drone are still at the top.
3. Turn toward The Block (left, −X) and toward The Board (right, +X). The ground is not one lawn: terraces and berms sit above or below the street.
4. In The Board, go to the lagoon edge around x 238, z 72. You should see one basin with a stone coping, not a stack of blue rings. Walk into it. The step should stop.
5. Look back at The Block. The train should already be moving, with riders in the cars, before you press Board.
6. Press Admit, then Block Coaster, then Board during a station dwell. If the train has already left, you wait one cycle. You should not be able to board it out on the course.
7. In a land plaza, away from the spine, guests should be standing or walking. They are not lined up down Main Street.
8. The Pocket pool, if you go find it, is one shape. The hub has no water.

Expected: the park is populated, the ground has depth, the shoreline is a shoreline, and the coaster is still a coaster.
